Read CLAUDE.md and the SO-101 section of docs/06_reuse_map.md. Bring the loop to my SO-101. I am at the robot. Ask me before any motion command runs for the first time.

1. SO101Backend implementing the Backend interface through LeRobot, with slow speed limits and a keyboard stop.
2. Calibrate: an AprilTag on the fixture, intrinsics from a checkerboard, and a hand-eye fit from 10 poses.
3. Task: the real version of T3. Insert the plug held in its jig into the target port on the mock panel. Fallback if the arm can't push hard enough: the 1U slide-in, checked by a reed switch or magnet sensor.
4. Have a frontier model write locate_port for this setup from 5 photos. Show me the code before it runs.
5. Run dance() with 30 samples, fit the regression, and show me the residual.
6. Checker: the connector's LED or the switch link light, read by a color threshold and cross-checked by Glimpse.
7. Record 10 attempts in a row to LOOPKIT_DATA/videos/so101_demo.mp4 and log traces.

Acceptance: 8 of 10, or an honest failure analysis by failure class. Write reports/S5.md.
