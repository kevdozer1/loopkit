## What ran

1. Skills in `loopkit/skills`, each a typed function whose signature and docstring generate its SkillSpec (closed params schema, outputs schema, pre and postconditions):
   - Perception: `locate(name)` for the box, plug, slot or rack, and `locate_port(n)`. Classical CV on the Observation only: the rack pose comes from a 3-DOF PnP fit to the 12 port features in `front` (dark open ports, yellow occupied plugs); the box from its magenta bezel plus its whole top face in `overhead`; the plug from its yellow body and green boot in `overhead`, with a silhouette fit. `read_panel()` asks the configured reader model through the router for the T1 report.
   - Motion: `move_to(pose)`, `approach(target, offset_mm)`, `retreat(distance_mm)`.
   - Manipulation: `grasp(part, pose)`, `release()`, `slide_into(target)`, `insert(target, depth_mm, speed_mm_s, max_force_n, search)` with a spiral search at the port mouth.
   - Calibration: `dance(target, n)` makes n small offset moves near the target, measures the image-space error in the wrist camera and fits a linear map from image error to pose correction. `aligned_insert(target)` applies it.
   - `done()` and `abort(reason)`.
2. Interpreter in `loopkit/runtime/interpreter.py`: resolves `{"ref": ...}` params from earlier outputs and the task params, retries in place, enforces `max_visits`, node timeouts, the task's time budget and a step limit, stops at once on a safety stop, and emits `node_start` and `node_end` events (the episode grader adds the `verdict`). A spend cap reached inside a skill ends the run as a budget stop, not a skill failure. `loopkit/runtime/so101.py` is a stub that raises NotImplementedError.
3. Program writer in `loopkit/program`: the prompt holds the TaskSpec's writer view, the skill catalog text, the 3 camera images, the graph format with one worked example on a different task, and earlier failure summaries. It asks for ProgramGraph JSON in json_schema mode, validates it and sends the validator's errors back up to 3 times.
4. Rehearse-and-patch in `loopkit/program/rehearse.py`, with episodes run and graded by `loopkit/sim/episode.py` in parallel processes. Failure summaries are text: failing node, error and outputs, checker metrics and what differed in the scene between failing and passing seeds. Every round is a `patch` event; the best graph is kept.
5. `uv run python -m loopkit.program.cli --model <key> --task T2` runs the acceptance procedure (10 training seeds, then held-out scoring) inside a spend session.
6. Tests: `tests/test_program.py` (interpreter semantics, repair with a scripted broken-then-fixed fake, the patch loop with a fake that improves, failure summaries, a simulator rehearsal), `tests/test_skills.py` (perception accuracy against simulator truth), and the privileged-state guard in `tests/test_sim.py`.

## The skill fix (1 of the 2 allowed rounds)

- What failed: on held-out seed 512 `slide_into` returned success with `seated: false`, 38.7 mm short of the back stop, and the graph went on to `done`. On training seed 103 (and dev seed 146) the box caught a rail on the way in and the contact force passed the 60 N safety limit.
- Cause: the box's position along its 200 mm width came from the magenta bezel strip alone, whose ends are a pixel or two each. Against simulator truth on seeds 100 to 159 its error was 0.37 mm std and 2.0 mm at worst, while the rails leave 1 mm per side.
- Fixes, all in skills: `locate(box)` takes the width position from the whole top face (0.07 mm std, 0.33 mm worst on the same seeds). `slide_into` enters the rails with a 25 N guard and retries at sideways offsets up to 1.4 mm, pushes again from 4 mm back if it stops short, and fails with an `alignment` error when the box does not reach the back stop. A safety stop now names the move it happened in.
- Honesty note: seed 512 is in the S3 held-out set, so the post-fix 20/20 on 500 to 519 is not a clean held-out number. The dev sweep (100 to 199) is not clean either: it includes seeds 103 and 146, which also showed the problem, and seeds 100 to 159 were used to measure the localization error. Neither number is a clean held-out result. The bake-off held-out seeds 2000 to 2019 have not been used.

```
find_box  = locate(name="box")                         retries 1   success -> pick_box,  failure -> give_up
pick_box  = grasp(part="box", pose=find_box.pose)      retries 1   success -> find_slot, failure -> give_up
find_slot = locate(name="slot", index=task.target_slot) retries 1  success -> slide,     failure -> give_up
slide     = slide_into(target=find_slot.pose)                      success -> let_go,    failure -> let_go
let_go    = release()                                              success -> back_off,  failure -> give_up
back_off  = retreat(distance_mm=80)                                success -> finish,    failure -> finish
finish    = done()                     give_up = abort(reason="a step failed")
```
