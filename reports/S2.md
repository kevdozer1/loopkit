# S2. Sim task suite: rack_cell

Date: 2026-10-17. Spend: $0 (no model calls).

## What ran

1. `loopkit/sim/rack_cell.py` builds the scene from the Menagerie Panda plus generated MJCF: a 4U rack section with 2 shelf slots (two L-rails each, with lead-in flares and a back stop), a patch panel with 12 ports, an LED above each port, a label strip texture with the numbers 1 to 12, and a parts tray holding the 1U box on a riser and the plug in a holder. `make_scene(seed)` applies the randomization from docs/02: rack pose (plus or minus 2 cm, plus or minus 3 deg), 2 to 4 lights, LED states, 0 to 6 occupied ports, target slot and port, and tray part jitter (plus or minus 5 mm, plus or minus 10 deg).
2. `loopkit/sim/backend.py`: `SimBackend` implements the Backend protocol. `reset(seed)`, `observe()` (3 cameras at 640 x 480 with intrinsics and extrinsics, joint state, gripper width and force, TCP pose, wrench estimate), `act()` (Cartesian moves through damped least-squares IK with min-jerk timing at 2 ms control ticks, joint moves, grip, wait), `stop()`. Commanded speed is clamped to 0.25 m/s. Leaving the workspace bounds or passing the 60 N contact force limit is a safety stop. The MuJoCo model and data are private; checkers and oracles use a documented `privileged` view.
3. `loopkit/sim/tasks.py`: TaskSpecs T1, T2, T3 with params schemas, budgets, safety limits and `nominal_geometry` (rack frame and tolerances, port and slot geometry, box and plug sizes, tray, appearance cues).
4. `loopkit/sim/checkers.py`: one checker per task, each returning a Verdict with metrics, failure_class and one evidence image.
5. `loopkit/sim/oracles.py`: privileged oracles for T1, T2 and T3, and a do-nothing program. CLI: `uv run python -m loopkit.sim.oracles --task T2 --seeds 0-49 --program oracle --workers 12 [--video]`.
6. `tests/test_sim.py` (18 tests): bit-for-bit determinism of the scene and the initial Observation, randomization ranges, the camera model (LED centres project onto LED pixels), TaskSpec round trips, safety stops, grip width and force, do-nothing scores 0, oracle passes, a wrong-port insertion is a `perception` failure, T1 field scoring, and the privileged-state guard.

## Contact settings

- Solver: timestep 1 ms, `implicitfast` integrator (Menagerie default), elliptic friction cone, `impratio` 10, `noslip_iterations` 3. Shadows are off in every light, since shadow rendering gave 1-level pixel differences between identical runs.
- Fingertip pads: friction 1.5, torsional 0.005, rolling 0.0001, `condim` 4, `solref` 0.002 1.
- Plug and port: plug friction 0.5, port bore 0.3, both `solref` 0.003 1, `solimp` 0.95 0.99 0.001. 45 deg mouth chamfer of 1.5 mm in the faceplate.
- Clearance: 0.5 mm total, as specified (plug 11.5 x 8.5 mm in a 12 x 9 mm opening). T3 passed at this clearance, so the 1.0 mm fallback was not needed.
- Gripper: a force-saturating position servo (gain 4000, damping 1200, force range plus or minus 140 N), so a closed grip holds 70 N per finger by default. Finger width is reported as the sum of both finger joints. Contact force for the safety limit and the wrench estimate come from constraint forces, not from accelerations.

## Scale and geometry

- 1U box: 150 mm deep x 200 mm wide x 44.45 mm high, 1.0 kg, gripped across its 44.45 mm height 12 mm from the back edge. Rails: 1 mm lateral clearance per side, back stop so 25 mm of the box stays proud of the rack.
- Patch panel: 12 ports at 16 mm pitch (the spec says about 15 mm; 16 mm leaves room for the mouth chamfer between ports), 12 x 9 mm opening, 12 mm deep bore, LED 11.5 mm above each port centre.
- Plug: 20 x 11.5 x 8.5 mm yellow body with a 16 x 3 x 1 mm key on top, plus a 25 mm long, 3 mm wide dark green boot behind the body as the grasp handle. Without the boot the fingers would have to grip the body itself and would reach the panel face before 8 mm of insertion. Occupied ports show the same yellow plug.
- Layout: the rack faces the robot at (0.60, 0.12) m with 50 deg yaw. That keeps every approach yaw 25 to 55 deg off the radial direction, which the Panda's wrist (joint 6) can reach without hitting its limit. The tray sits at (0.46, -0.37) m with the box on a 180 mm riser and the plug in a 195 mm holder, so the hand clears the tray rim. The plug holder was moved out of the slot 1 approach corridor after oracle failures on seeds 100 to 139.
- Cameras: `overhead` at (0.50, -0.25, 1.15) m looking straight down, 40 deg field of view; `front` 42 cm in front of the panel and 25 cm above the port row (about 49 cm from the ports), aimed at the panel centre, 30 deg; `wrist` on the hand, 60 deg, tilted 14 deg toward the fingers.

## Privileged-state guard

`test_observation_carries_no_object_pose` pins the Observation fields (cameras, joint state, gripper, TCP pose, wrench, sim time) and checks a dumped Observation names no box, plug, rack, port or slot. `test_backend_hides_mujoco_state` checks SimBackend exposes no `model`, `data` or `scene`. `test_skills_cannot_reach_privileged_state` parses every module in `loopkit/skills`, `loopkit/runtime` and `loopkit/program`: skills and the interpreter may import nothing from `loopkit.sim` (or mujoco) and may not name `privileged`, `PrivilegedView`, `_scene` or `_arm`; the program package may import only `loopkit.sim.tasks` and the harness's episode grader `loopkit.sim.episode`, whose plain-data record is all it sees. A second test checks the guard catches a skill that peeks.
